EidGlitz

A motion video made for the EidGlitz team, promoting EidGlitz Ibadan Festival 7.0, 2026.

Role

Motion & Video Designer

Scope

Motion Graphics · Video Editing

Tools

Canva

EidGlitz is an annual Eid festival in Ibadan, now in its 7th edition, bringing together teenagers, youths, and adults for a day of games, connection, and halal fun. The EidGlitz team brought me on to create a motion video promoting the 2026 edition of the festival.

I built it in Canva, keeping the pacing quick and leaning on the festival's own colors and energy, something made to grab attention on a fast scroll through Instagram or TikTok.
